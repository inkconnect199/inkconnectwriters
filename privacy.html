Privacy Policy

This page explains what information InkConnect may handle and how it is used.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Information we handle

Depending on how you use InkConnect, information may include your name, username, email address, phone number, country, account role, profile details and photo, referral information, and messages you send us. Marketplace activity can include job posts, applications, messages between users, reviews, and submitted work.

Payment activity may include transaction references, amounts, status, and payment channel. Payment providers may collect and process payment details directly under their own privacy terms.

Identity verification is required for all new client and writer accounts. We collect the name and number on your national ID or driver's license, plus images of the front and back, with your consent. These documents are stored separately from your public profile. Images are kept in a private Google Drive folder controlled by the service owner and retrieved for administrators through authenticated review actions; authorized storage administrators may also have direct access.

How we use information

We use information to operate accounts and marketplace features, connect clients and writers, process and record payments, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, and maintain the service.

Identity documents are reviewed manually by an administrator. InkConnect does not use this feature for automated document authenticity checks, facial recognition, or biometric matching. The current app does not automatically delete ID records, so the service operator must define and apply a retention and deletion schedule before collecting real documents.

Some profile and marketplace information is visible to other users so they can evaluate profiles, jobs, applications, and reviews. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in public profile fields or messages.

Storage and service providers

The current project configuration uses a browser-based demo database when the live backend URL is not configured. In that mode, demo records and session information are stored in your browser. Identity documents must not be submitted in demo mode. A deployed version stores ID details in its Google Sheet and document images in a private Google Drive folder, accessible through authenticated administrator actions. The Sheet, Drive folder, and Apps Script project must be restricted to authorized personnel. Other configured storage, payment, and infrastructure providers process information under their own terms.

The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may contact Google to retrieve those font files.

Cookies and browser storage

The app uses browser local storage and session storage for demo data, sign-in state, and site preferences. These are stored on your device and can be cleared through your browser settings; clearing them may sign you out or remove local demo data. This page's cookie information is included here because the current app uses browser storage rather than a separate cookie-policy page.

Retention and your choices

You can keep your account details accurate and contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion of personal information. We may need to retain some records to operate the service, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. In demo mode, you can clear local site data in your browser.

Changes and contact

Privacy practices can depend on which features and providers are enabled in a particular deployment. We may update this notice when those practices change. For privacy questions or requests, email support@inkconnect.com.

This notice is an overview of the current application, not legal advice. Before operating publicly, the business should review it for the jurisdictions where it operates and the final data flows of its deployment.
