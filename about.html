Our story

Writing is craft. Hiring shouldn't be a gamble.

InkConnect was built to make freelance writing collaboration fast, fair and trustworthy — for clients who need words that work, and writers who deserve to get paid on time.

🤝

Trust by design

Every payment runs through escrow, so writers know funds exist before they start, and clients only release payment once work is delivered.

🔍

Verified talent

Writers build a track record of ratings and completed jobs right on their profile — no guesswork required.

⚡

Fast to start

Post a job or apply to one in minutes. Messaging, applications and payments all live in one dashboard.

Our mission

Give every writer a fair shot, and every client a reliable partner.

We started InkConnect after watching too many great writers get underpaid on generic gig platforms, and too many clients struggle to find someone who could actually write. So we built a marketplace focused on one craft, done well.
